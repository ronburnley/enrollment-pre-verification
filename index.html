Why is this required now?
Starting with Plan Year 2028, federal rules require every consumer to actively verify their eligibility information before receiving premium subsidies. Automatic renewal with subsidies is no longer allowed, so completing this once per year keeps your financial help in place.

For 2028, an employer plan is considered affordable if your share of the premium for self-only coverage is less than about 9% of your household income. If it costs more than that, you may still qualify for marketplace savings.
Why do I need to provide this?
If you already have qualifying coverage, like an affordable employer plan or Medicare, you usually can't get marketplace subsidies on top of it. Answering these questions keeps you from picking a plan you'd have to pay full price for.
